# Product Overview

RewardLoop is a customer retention platform that helps local businesses increase repeat customers through a simple digital loyalty wallet.

Instead of paper loyalty cards or manual notebooks, businesses identify customers using their mobile number, automatically reward purchases, and securely redeem rewards using OTP verification.

Customers do not need to install an application.

RewardLoop is designed to become part of the business's normal billing workflow rather than a separate loyalty system.

---

# Problem Statement

Most local businesses rely heavily on repeat customers but lack affordable tools to retain them.

Common problems include:

- No customer database
- No visit history
- No digital loyalty program
- Manual notebooks
- Paper loyalty cards
- No repeat customer tracking
- No customer engagement

Customers also face problems:

- Lost loyalty cards
- Forgotten rewards
- No visibility into reward balance
- No consistent loyalty experience

---

# Our Solution

RewardLoop transforms customer loyalty into a simple digital workflow.

Every transaction becomes an opportunity to:

- Identify the customer
- Record the visit
- Award rewards
- Redeem rewards securely
- Build customer history

The entire experience is designed to complete within seconds.

# Core Product

RewardLoop consists of six primary capabilities.

---

## Customer Identity

Every customer is identified using their mobile number.

The phone number becomes the customer's digital identity within a business.

---

## Business Catalog

Businesses can manage their service catalog:

**MVP: Services only**

Examples:

Salon

- Haircut
- Beard
- Hair Spa

Car Wash

- Foam Wash
- Interior Cleaning

Gym

- Gym Session
- Personal Training

> **Phase 2:** Product catalog (physical items like shampoo, protein powder) will be added in Phase 2. See `00_Founder_Decisions.md` Decision 24.

The same platform works across industries.

---

## Digital Reward Wallet

Customers automatically earn rewards after every completed transaction.

Rewards remain linked to the customer's mobile number.

Reward redemption is protected using OTP verification.

---

## Customer History

Businesses can view:

- Previous visits
- Previous transactions
- Reward balance
- Last visit

Customer information becomes available automatically when the phone number is entered.

---

## Billing Workflow

RewardLoop combines:

- Customer lookup
- Billing
- Reward calculation
- Payment
- Loyalty

into one continuous workflow.

---

## Business Insights

Businesses can monitor:

- Today's Revenue
- Today's Customer Count
- Today's Rewards Given

using simple KPI cards with bottom sheet drill-downs.

> **MVP scope:** Insights show **today's data only** (per `00_Founder_Decisions.md` Decision 17).
> Multi-period summaries (yesterday, weekly, monthly) are Phase 2.
