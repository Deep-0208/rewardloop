# Premium SaaS UX

## Dashboards & Analytics

- **Visual Hierarchy (Stripe):** Clear separation of metrics. Large KPI numbers, subtle muted labels, green/red trend indicators.
- **Loading UX:** Always use Skeleton loaders that mimic the final layout geometry instead of generic spinners.

## Tables & Forms

- **Data Tables (Notion/Linear):** High-density tables, sticky headers, subtle row hover effects, and contextual action menus (ellipsis).
- **Forms (Clerk):** Clear validation states, inline error messages, and save-on-blur for rapid data entry. Avoid modal forms for complex data; use dedicated pages or sliding side-panels.
